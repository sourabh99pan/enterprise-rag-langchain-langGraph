Company Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Meal Expenses
Employees travelling for approved business purposes may claim meal expenses up to ₹800 per day.

2. Hotel Expenses
Hotel expenses are reimbursable up to ₹4,000 per night for domestic business travel. Expenses above this limit require prior written approval from the department head.

3. Travel Approval
Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking tickets or accommodation.

4. Submission Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 15 calendar days after the completion of the business trip.

5. Receipts
Employees must attach itemized receipts for all individual expenses above ₹500. Claims without required receipts may be rejected.

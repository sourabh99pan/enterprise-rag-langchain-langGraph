Company IT Security Policy

1. Passwords
Employees must use passwords containing at least 12 characters, including uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.

2. Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all company email accounts and remote access systems.

3. Device Security
Employees must lock their computers whenever they leave their desks. Company laptops must use approved endpoint protection software.

4. Data Sharing
Confidential company information must not be shared with unauthorized external parties. Employees must use company-approved file-sharing tools for business documents.

5. Security Incidents
Suspected phishing emails or security incidents must be reported to the IT Security team immediately.

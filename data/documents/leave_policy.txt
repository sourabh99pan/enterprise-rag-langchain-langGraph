Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance and approved by the employee's manager.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of sick leave per calendar year. For sick leave lasting more than 2 consecutive working days, employees must submit a medical certificate.

3. Emergency Leave
Employees may request emergency leave for urgent personal situations. The manager must be informed as soon as possible.

4. Carry Forward
Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days to the next calendar year. Unused sick leave cannot be carried forward.
